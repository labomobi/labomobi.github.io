Breed medaka, fill your collection

How to Play
🐟

かけあわせ

Breeding

2ひきを選ぶと子メダカが生まれます。親の遺伝子から、どんな柄になるかはお楽しみ。

Pick two fish and a baby is born. Its pattern comes from its parents' genes — you never know what you'll get.

📖

図鑑あつめ

Collection Book

生まれた品種は図鑑に登録。光る幹之やラメなど、レアな柄を集めよう。

New varieties go into your collection book. Hunt for rare ones that glow or sparkle.

🌿

お世話

Care

エサやりと水換え。水草やエアポンプを置くと、水がきれいに保てます。

Feed your fish and change the water. Plants and air pumps keep it clean longer.

🏠

環境を変える

Your Aquarium

コインで金魚鉢から水槽へ。底砂や置物で、自分だけの水辺をつくろう。

Upgrade from a fishbowl to a tank with coins, and decorate it with gravel and ornaments.
